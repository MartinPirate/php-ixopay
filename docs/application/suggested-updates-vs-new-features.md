# Suggested Updates vs New Features

This note separates public-capability improvements from genuinely new proposals.

## Suggested updates

These areas appear to exist publicly already, so the right pitch is improvement,
not pretending IXOPAY lacks them.

### 1. Sandbox workflow upgrade

Public evidence:

- Testing guide:
  https://documentation.ixopay.com/docs/guides/getting-started/testing
- 3-D Secure testing:
  https://documentation.ixopay.com/docs/reference/features/3d-secure/testing

Why it is an update:

- IXOPAY already documents sandbox testing and simulation paths
- the improvement angle is onboarding, scenario setup, replay, and clarity

### 2. Hosted payment page UI upgrade

Public evidence:

- Hosted payment pages reference:
  https://documentation.ixopay.com/docs/reference/integration/processing-options/hosted-payment-pages
- Hosted payment pages guide:
  https://documentation.ixopay.com/docs/guides/getting-started/accept-payments/hosted-payment-pages

Why it is an update:

- hosted payment pages clearly exist publicly
- the opportunity is better design quality, motion, trust cues, and conversion UX

### 3. Payment selection page card system

Public evidence:

- Payment method selection:
  https://documentation.ixopay.com/docs/reference/features/payment-method-selection
- Multi-Method Connector:
  https://documentation.ixopay.com/manual/docs/connector/multi-method-connector

Why it is an update:

- payment selection is documented publicly
- the improvement angle is modern card layouts, clearer method comparison, and regional ordering

### 4. Result and confirmation template pack expansion

Public evidence:

- FAST Editor:
  https://documentation.ixopay.com/manual/docs/fast
- Pay-by-Link:
  https://documentation.ixopay.com/docs/guides/features/pay-by-link

Why it is an update:

- templating is documented publicly
- the gap looks more like breadth and polish than total absence

### 5. Africa-focused recipe expansion

Public evidence:

- Recipes landing:
  https://documentation.ixopay.com/docs/recipes
- "How to" landing:
  https://documentation.ixopay.com/docs/recipes/how-to

Why it is an update:

- recipes exist publicly
- the opportunity is market-specific launch patterns and fallback guidance

## New features

These are the areas I could not clearly verify as existing public product surfaces.

### 1. Receipt template system and gallery

Why this is new:

- I found public evidence for templates and result pages
- I did not find a clear public receipt gallery or receipt design system

### 2. Receipt variants by merchant vertical

Why this is new:

- travel, retail, subscriptions, and marketplaces need different proof-of-payment layouts
- I did not find a public product surface showing this as a structured feature

### 3. Developer guide agent

Why this is new:

- I did not find a public IXOPAY assistant that maps goals to docs, adapters, and test flows

### 4. Smart payment recommendations

Why this is new:

- I did not find a public recommendation layer for payment methods and routing presets

## Expansion

### African payment expansion pack

This should be framed as expansion rather than claimed absence.

Why:

- IXOPAY continuously adds adapters and regional methods publicly through release notes
- the strongest pitch is broader African-market coverage plus recipes and launch playbooks
